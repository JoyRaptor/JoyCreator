## What you may do

- Use, copy, change and share the **code** under the GPL v3 ([LICENSE](LICENSE)).
- Say truthfully that something "works with Joy Creator", "is a fork of Joy Creator" or "is built from Joy Creator's
  source", and link to this project.
- Write reviews, tutorials and articles that use the names to talk about the app.

## What you may not do without asking

- Name your own app, company, store listing, domain or social account Joy Creator, JoyBrush or Joybot, or something
  confusingly close.
- Use the Joy Creator icon or artwork as the icon of a different app.
- Suggest that a modified version is the official one, or that the owner endorses it.

A fork must use its own name and icon. Where a fork's About screen credits its roots, "Built on Joy Creator" is fine.
