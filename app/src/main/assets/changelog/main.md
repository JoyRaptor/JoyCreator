# Welcome to Joy Creator beta
## Your work lives in your Joy Creator folder
- Everything you make is kept in one folder you choose, and removing the app never loses it
- Recordings and exports land there with readable names
- Share to Joy Creator from any app

---

## JoyBrush
- As many drawings as you like, with pictures in the Lobby

---

## Studio
- Big files from elsewhere are linked; Copy, Move or Leave them whenever you like. A yellow dot shows what's outside your folder
- One Trash for projects, drawings and files, with Undo right after deleting a project
- Space (Settings → Storage Location) shows what takes room and frees some up

---

## Joybot
- Replies as he types, works with any OpenAI-compatible service, and keeps your key encrypted
