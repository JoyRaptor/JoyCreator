Joy Creator

A creative studio on your phone. Paint and animate, edit video, make music visuals and film-poster title looks, build characters, and keep everything in one folder you choose. Free for good: no ads, no subscription, no watermark, no account.

Help test itPrivacy policy

What is inside

  • JoyBrush: painting and animation with brushes made to feel real.
  • Studio: a video and audio editor with titles, effects, transitions and captions.
  • VisLab: music visualisers built from parts you can mix.
  • TextLab: title looks like chrome, gold, neon and fire.
  • SpriteLab: sprite sheets, characters and puppets.
  • Joybot: an AI helper that knows the whole app. You bring your own key; it is optional.

Joy Creator is in beta and is coming to Google Play. This page will grow into the full site; until then it holds the privacy policy and the tester sign-up.
