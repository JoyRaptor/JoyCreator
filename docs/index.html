A creative studio on your phone

Paint it. Animate it. Cut it. Score it.

Joy Creator puts painting, animation, video editing, music visuals, film-poster titles and characters in one app, and keeps everything you make in one folder you choose. Free for good: no ads, no subscription, no watermark, no account.

Joybot, your helper

Joybot knows the whole app. Ask it how to do something, or ask it to do the job. It changes your project only in steps you can undo, and it uses your own key from the AI service you like, so the rest of Joy Creator never needs one. More about Joybot.

Yours to keep

  • One folder, chosen by you. Open it with any file manager, back it up, move it.
  • Removing the app never deletes your work.
  • No analytics, no tracking, and I never receive your work. The privacy policy lists every case.
  • Open source (GNU GPL v3).
