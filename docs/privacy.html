Privacy Policy — Joy Creator

Last updated: [DATE OF PUBLISHING — owner]

Joy Creator is a creative studio for your phone: drawing and animation (JoyBrush), video and audio editing (Studio), music visualisers (VisLab), title text looks (TextLab), sprites and avatars (SpriteLab), recording, and Joybot, an AI helper. It is made by an independent artist. This page says plainly what stays on your phone and what, if anything, leaves it.

The short version

  • Your work stays on your phone, in the Joy Creator folder you choose. Removing the app never deletes that folder.
  • No account is needed. No ads. No analytics, crash reporting or tracking. Nothing about you is sold or shared.
  • Joy Creator has no servers of its own and does not send your work to us. (An earlier version of the code this app grew from could talk to its original author's servers; that part was removed from Joy Creator in October 2026.)
  • Something leaves your phone only when you use a feature that needs the internet, listed below.

What stays on your phone

Projects, drawings, recordings, exports, pictures you bring in, looks and packs, and your settings. Projects and drawings are kept in your Joy Creator folder (a folder you pick); the app also keeps working copies and caches in its own storage, which Android deletes when the app is removed. Joy Creator never uploads your work on its own.

Joy Creator does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.

Permissions

Joy Creator asks for a permission only to do the thing it is named for: the camera and microphone to record, location only if you turn on location tags, notifications to show recording and export progress, display over other apps for the floating recording and visualiser controls, battery optimisation exemption so a long recording is not stopped, and access to the folder you choose (and media you pick) so your work can be saved and opened there. Android asks you at the moment you use each feature.

Your control

  • Everything Joy Creator makes is a file in your folder: open it, move it, back it up or delete it with any file manager. Deleted items go to the app's Trash first.
  • Removing the app deletes its own storage, never your Joy Creator folder.
  • To stop anything leaving your phone: don't add an AI key or service address, leave location, weather and Remote off, and don't open What's New or check for updates.

Children

Joy Creator is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
