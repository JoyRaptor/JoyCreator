Joy Creator is a creative studio for your phone. Paint and animate, cut video, make music visuals and film-poster titles, build characters, and keep all of it in one folder you choose.

FREE, WITH NOTHING HIDDEN
No ads, no subscription, no watermark, no export paywall, no account. Your work stays on your phone, in your own folder, and removing the app never deletes it.

WHAT IS INSIDE
• JoyBrush: paint and animate with pencil, oil, watercolour and other brushes made to feel like the real thing, with layers and frames.
• Studio: edit video and audio on a timeline with titles, effects, transitions, speech captions and export.
• VisLab: build music visualisers from parts you can mix and tweak, and use them in your videos.
• TextLab: title text with looks like chrome, gold, neon or fire, ready to use in your videos.
• SpriteLab: sprite sheets, characters and puppets you can move.
• Capture: record with the camera or the screen.
• Joybot: an AI helper who knows the whole app. It uses your own key from the AI service you like (the rest of the app works without one), and it only changes your project in steps you can undo.

MADE FOR ONE HAND
Everything is built for a phone first.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No analytics and no tracking. Something leaves your phone only when you choose a feature that needs the internet, such as asking Joybot with your own key. The privacy policy lists each case.

Joy Creator is open source (GPL v3) and is built on FadCam by FadSec Lab.

Joy Creator is in beta: some features are newer than others and are still being tried on real phones.
