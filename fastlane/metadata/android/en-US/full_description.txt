<p><b>Joy Creator</b> is a creative studio for your phone. Paint and animate, cut video, make music visuals and film-poster titles, build characters, and keep all of it in one folder you choose.</p><p><b>Free, with nothing hidden:</b> no ads, no subscription, no watermark, no export paywall, no account. Your work stays on your phone, in your own folder, and removing the app never deletes it.</p><p><b>What is inside</b></p><p>&bull; <b>JoyBrush</b>: paint and animate with pencil, oil, watercolour and other brushes made to feel like the real thing, with layers and frames.<br>&bull; <b>Studio</b>: edit video and audio on a timeline with titles, effects, transitions, speech captions and export.<br>&bull; <b>VisLab</b>: build music visualisers from parts you can mix and tweak, and use them in your videos.<br>&bull; <b>TextLab</b>: title text with looks like chrome, gold, neon or fire, ready to use in your videos.<br>&bull; <b>SpriteLab</b>: sprite sheets, characters and puppets you can move.<br>&bull; <b>Capture</b>: record with the camera or the screen.<br>&bull; <b>Joybot</b>: an AI helper who knows the whole app. Bring your own key from the AI service you like (or none at all: the app works without it). Joybot only changes your project in steps you can undo.</p><p><b>Made for one hand.</b> Everything is built for a phone first.</p><p><b>Private by design.</b> No analytics and no tracking. Something leaves your phone only when you choose a feature that needs the internet, such as asking Joybot with your own key. The privacy policy lists each case.</p><p>Joy Creator is open source (GPL v3) and is built on FadCam by FadSec Lab.</p><p><i>Joy Creator is in beta: some features are newer than others and are still being tried on real phones.</i></p>
