# Line preset catalogue

The ten presets in VECTOR_RENDER_DESIGN section 2. Files are validated by
LinePresetFilesTest through BrushJson.decodeChecked. They are not extra drawer
entries yet; the lead's editable-line shelf will choose them by their distinct IDs.

Pencils inherit the current shipped paper/contact model. This is preset setup,
not an owner-approved Proko tuning pass. The watercolour preset preserves the
approved POC settings and image set; its pressure taper lives in the watercolour
section, without unsupported generic dash/taper shaping. Wire's screen-width
behaviour needs the lead's sharp look levels, rather than baking a pixel stroke.
