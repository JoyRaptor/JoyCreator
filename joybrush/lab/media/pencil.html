Pencil tuner

The app's own pencil engine. Every change redraws the test strokes. Turn the test strokes off to draw with your pen. A dot (•) marks a setting you changed.

Pen: pressure and tilt curves

Drag the two handles. Across is what the pen reads; up is what the pencil feels. Double-tap a box to make it straight again. These are the same curves as the app's brush settings.
