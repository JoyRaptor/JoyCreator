## 5. The lesson this session keeps teaching

Every serious defect today was found by MEASURING, not by reading:

- The silence was found in `dumpsys audio` (`state:idle` while the log claimed success)
- The stutter was found in logcat (`drift desync err=-126 -> repark`, every tick)
- The AudioTrack storm was found by counting `new player piid:` lines
- The build corruption was found by listing gradle daemons (two were running)

And every one of them would have been caught in the first three seconds of the acceptance
runs the specs already asked for. **The acceptance sections are not paperwork.** Running
them is cheaper than any of the debugging above.
