## BUG — music/audio kept playing after the video stopped — FIXED + device-verified (2026-06-22, work phone REAL_SERIAL)
Repro (confirmed via `dumpsys audio`): play a project where the audio/music track is longer than the video;
when the video reaches the end, the video freezes (button → play) but the music **MediaPlayer stayed
`state:started`** and kept playing. Root cause: `syncAudioPlayerWithPlayhead` (runs every playhead tick, which
runs continuously while the activity is resumed) gated on `playerManager.getPlayWhenReady()`, which stays

imagePlaybackActive || audioTailActive` — with a 400ms debounce (`audioStoppedSinceMs`) so the music doesn't
dip during brief clip-boundary buffering but IS paused once playback truly stops. Also added explicit
`pauseAudioPlayer()` at the video-end "fully stop" branch and the STATE_ENDED handler, added
`FaditorPlayerManager.isEnded()`, and made the play/pause onClick handle the audio-tail FIRST (so tapping
pause during a tail stops the music regardless of clip type). VERIFIED: mid-playback music plays (started);
after the video ends, no audio is `started`.

## LOOSE END / P1 — Audio track volume + VOLUME KEYFRAMES — DONE (2026-06-21, compile-green + installed)
Implemented end-to-end:
- **Model** (`AudioClip`): `VolumeKeyframe{timeMs,volume}` + sorted `List` + `addOrUpdateVolumeKeyframe()`
  (40ms tolerance merge), `gainAtClipMs()` (linear interp, flat-held outside ends), `hasVolumeKeyframes()`,
  deep-copied in the copy ctor. Time is **clip-local** (0 = clip start on timeline).
- **Storage** (`ProjectStorage`): `volumeKeyframes:[{t,v}]` per audio clip, `has()`-guarded (back-compat).
- **Keyframe mode** (`FaditorEditorActivity`): **long-press the Volume tool** with an audio clip selected
  toggles `audioVolumeKeyframeMode` (toast + the Volume icon turns into a **green "timer"** glyph = armed,
  grey/normal = off). When armed, the volume sheet's `onVolumeChanged` writes a keyframe at the playhead
  (clip-local) + applies it live + redraws, instead of setting whole-clip volume.
- **Playback**: `applyAudioKeyframeGains()` runs each playhead tick — for any playing audio clip with
  keyframes it sets `MediaPlayer` volume to the interpolated gain → linear fades between keyframes.
- **Export** (`VolumeAudioProcessor` + `ExportManager`): the processor gained a `setVolumeEnvelope(times,vols)`
  that interpolates gain per audio frame (tracks sampleRate/channels/framePosition; resets on flush). The
  audio-clip export path sets the envelope when keyframes exist (else the old static volume).
- **Timeline** (`EditorTimelineView.drawAudioTrack`): a **light-blue rubber-band envelope** (`volEnvLine/
  DotPaint`) drawn keyframe-to-keyframe across the audio clip rect — x = clip-local time, y = gain (0..2,
  bottom..top, 100% at mid), with dots at each keyframe + flat holds past the ends.
Whole-track volume (mode OFF) was already working and is unchanged.
