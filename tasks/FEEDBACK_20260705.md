  FIX (JoyRaptor): in the sprite keyframe drop path, if a keyframe exists at that frame/tolerance → replace its
  sprite ref instead of inserting; keep it one undo step. (Mirrors the caption-style-keyframe replace-at-

- **Butt preview still imperfect:** as the item nears objects on ANOTHER layer, the landing preview to the

  moving object on exactly ONE row (the hovered target) and nowhere else.
