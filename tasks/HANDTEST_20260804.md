## 1. The dislodge (the headline gesture)

| do | expect |
|---|---|
| Press and hold a clip on the **main track** | it lifts: a **purple halo** appears around it with an **upward chevron** above it, and the phone buzzes |
| Keep holding, then **pull UP** | the clip leaves the main track and appears as a layer above it; the main track closes the gap |
| **Undo** | the clip returns to the main track, in its original position, with its sound |

**Then the two things that must NOT happen:**

| do | expect |
|---|---|
| Hold a clip, then slide **sideways** | the halo disappears and nothing else happens — no reorder dialog on release. This is the "precision horizontal move" case |
| Hold a clip and **release without moving** | the reorder dialog opens, exactly as it used to |

### 1b. The hand-over (NEW, and the one most likely to be wrong)

After the pull-up, the clip should **keep following your finger** instead of dropping where it was.

| do | expect |
|---|---|
| Hold a main-track clip, pull **up**, and **keep moving without lifting** | the clip stays under your finger and moves with it — horizontally in time, vertically between layers |
| Watch the instant it leaves the spine | it must **not jump sideways**. If it hops horizontally the moment it lifts, that is the grab-offset bug and it is the finding |
| Drag to the far left/right edge and hold | the timeline should auto-pan |
| Lift your finger | it drops where it looks like it will drop, and **one undo** returns it to the main track |
| Pull up and lift immediately, without moving | it should still land as a layer — not open a menu |

If the clip demotes but then sits still and ignores the finger, adoption failed. That is a
**degraded** outcome, not a broken one — drag it normally as a second gesture and note it.

### 1c. Dragging a clip back ONTO the main track (the insertion highlight, NEW)

| do | expect |
|---|---|
| Hold a **video or image clip on a layer** until it lifts, then drag it down over the **main track** | a **solid purple line** appears at the seam between two clips — where it will go |
| Slide left and right along the main track | the line **jumps from seam to seam**, always showing the nearest one. It should never sit in the middle of a clip |
| Release | the clip lands **exactly at the line**, not somewhere else |
| **Undo once** | it goes back to the layer it came from |
| Try the same with a **text box** | **no purple line at all** — text is not allowed on the main track, so it must not be offered |

To capture evidence, run this while you test and send me the output:

```bash
adb -s SANDBOX_SERIAL logcat -d -s SPINEDROP
```

It prints the seam the line promised (`hover seam=`) and the seam the drop used (`DROP seam=`).
**Those two numbers must match.** If they differ, that is the finding and the numbers are the proof.

## 3. The mask (this one is checking a bug is gone)

| do | expect |
|---|---|
| Long-press a picture-in-picture clip → **Mask** | the dialog opens with sliders |
| Press **BACK** without touching anything | **no hole is left in the clip.** Before today, backing out left a permanent 30%×20% hole |
| Open it again, move sliders, press **Cancel** | the clip is unchanged |
| Open it, move sliders, press **OK**, then **Undo** | the mask is removed in one press |

## 4. Text style (the new controls)

| do | expect |
|---|---|
| Double-tap a text box in the preview → scroll to **STYLE** | Outline / Glow / Shadow sliders and a Background plate row |
| Drag **Outline** up | the outline appears immediately — it should NOT need a colour picked first |

## Also worth a look while you are in there

- **Tap-to-seek should now be noticeably quicker.** The first attempt was reverted for making it
  worse; the real cause turned out to be that a plain tap was running the frame-accurate *settle*
  meant for drag-scrubbing, costing two extra keyframe decodes on top of its own. A tap no longer
  does that. **It should be no less accurate** — the tap's own seek was always exact. If it feels
  less precise when lining up a cut, that is the finding.
- **The captions picker** should stay out of the way until you tap CC or select a caption.
- **Deleting a clip that has text sitting on it** should ask what to do with the text, and the
  whole thing should be **one** undo press.
