# Hand test — 2026-08-04 build

Four things `adb` cannot test, because its `input swipe` interpolates movement over the whole
gesture and so cannot express **hold still, THEN move**. Everything else this session was verified
on device or in a harness.

Each step says what SHOULD happen. If something else happens, that is the finding — note what you
saw rather than retrying until it works.

---

## 1. The dislodge (the headline gesture)

| do | expect |
|---|---|
| Press and hold a clip on the **main track** | it lifts: a **purple halo** appears around it with an **upward chevron** above it, and the phone buzzes |
| Keep holding, then **pull UP** | the clip leaves the main track and appears as a layer above it; the main track closes the gap |
| **Undo** | the clip returns to the main track, in its original position, with its sound |

**Then the two things that must NOT happen:**

| do | expect |
|---|---|
| Hold a clip, then slide **sideways** | the halo disappears and nothing else happens — no reorder dialog on release. This is the "precision horizontal move" case |
| Hold a clip and **release without moving** | the reorder dialog opens, exactly as it used to |

## 2. Double-tap

| do | expect |
|---|---|
| Double-tap a clip on the main track | the reorder dialog opens |
| Note the playhead | it **will** have moved to where you tapped. That is deliberate, not a bug |
| Back out of the dialog | the playhead is back where it was, with no visible jump |

## 3. The mask (this one is checking a bug is gone)

| do | expect |
|---|---|
| Long-press a picture-in-picture clip → **Mask** | the dialog opens with sliders |
| Press **BACK** without touching anything | **no hole is left in the clip.** Before today, backing out left a permanent 30%×20% hole |
| Open it again, move sliders, press **Cancel** | the clip is unchanged |
| Open it, move sliders, press **OK**, then **Undo** | the mask is removed in one press |

## 4. Text style (the new controls)

| do | expect |
|---|---|
| Double-tap a text box in the preview → scroll to **STYLE** | Outline / Glow / Shadow sliders and a Background plate row |
| Drag **Outline** up | the outline appears immediately — it should NOT need a colour picked first |
| Press **Cancel** | the text looks exactly as it did before you opened the dialog |
| Do it again, press **OK**, then **Undo** | one press undoes the whole style change |

## Also worth a look while you are in there

- **Tap-to-seek** should feel noticeably quicker than it did. It now seeks fast and lands on the
  exact frame a moment later. Tell me if it feels *less accurate* — that would mean the settle is
  not landing.
- **The captions picker** should stay out of the way until you tap CC or select a caption.
- **Deleting a clip that has text sitting on it** should ask what to do with the text, and the
  whole thing should be **one** undo press.
