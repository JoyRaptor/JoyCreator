# Test Joy Creator with me

*Source of the page `docs/testers.html`. Placeholders in [BRACKETS IN CAPITALS] need the owner. Written 2026-11-14.*

**Joy Creator is a free creative studio for your Android phone**: paint and animate, edit video, make music visuals and title looks, build characters, and keep it all in one folder you choose. No ads, no subscription, no watermark.

Before it goes public, Google asks me to have **12 people try it for two weeks**. That's you. Thank you.

## What I'm asking

- Install it from the link below and **keep it installed for 14 days in a row**. Google counts that.
- **Open it a few times** over the two weeks. A few minutes each time is fine.
- Try the ten-step checklist when you have a quiet 20 minutes (below), and tell me what confused you or broke.

You don't need to know anything technical. If something is confusing, that is exactly what I want to hear.

## How to join (about 3 minutes)

1. **Use an Android phone or tablet** with a Google account (Gmail).
2. **Send me your Gmail address**: [HOW TO REACH THE OWNER: EMAIL OR MESSAGE]. I add you to the tester list.
3. **Open the test link on your phone**: [PLAY OPT-IN LINK]. Tap **Become a tester**.
4. Tap the link to **install it from the Play Store** (it can take a few minutes after you join before the button appears).
5. **Open Joy Creator.** It asks you to pick a folder for your work: that's where everything you make is kept. Removing the app never deletes it.
6. That's it. Come back to it now and then over the next two weeks.

## The ten-step checklist

Stop at any step that is confusing and write down what you expected.

1. **First open.** Did it open? Were you asked to pick a folder, and did you understand why?
2. **The lobby.** Can you tell where to go to draw, to edit a video, to make a visual? Which door did you try first?
3. **Draw.** Open JoyBrush, make a drawing with two brushes, undo once, close the app, open it again: is your drawing there?
4. **Make a short video.** Open Studio, add a video or picture, add a title, and press Export. Did the file land where you expected? Does it play?
5. **Library.** Find your drawing and your video in the Library. Hold one: does the menu make sense?
6. **Music visual.** Open VisLab, pick a look, play it, change one thing.
7. **A title look.** Open TextLab, pick a look for a word of your own, and put it on a title in Studio.
8. **Joybot (optional).** Open Joybot and ask "What can I do here?". Joybot's AI needs a key from an AI service you choose; skip it if you don't have one.
9. **Interrupt it.** Switch apps or take a call in the middle of drawing or editing, then come back: is your work still there?
10. **Leave and return.** Close the app. A day later, open it: is everything where you left it?
