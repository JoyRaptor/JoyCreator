## Round 3 (11-16, [report](JoyCreator_Audit_Round3_20261116.md)): 15 findings, 14 optimizations and 1 correctness bug, none high

All 15 go to one **Sonnet** lane (the owner's rule: cheaper models for findings that aren't hard). The same verify-first rule applies. For an optimization, measure before and after on the Note 9; if the gain is too small to notice, record "not worth it" and move on. JoyBrush items (R3-JB-01 eyedropper, R3-JB-02 fill-pen) touch the canvas, so name the function to the engine Fable first. TextLab items: the labs-polish lane is done, so they're free.
