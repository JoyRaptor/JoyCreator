JoyBrush papers: how small can they go?

The 45 new papers take 205.7 MB as they are today. Each paper is three pictures: the colour you see, the tooth a pencil or brush feels, and the water map watercolour reads. Pick one option for each part. The left picture is always today's full-size paper; the right one is your pick. The bar at the bottom adds up the size for all 45.

Pictures are drawn one paper pixel to one screen pixel, as the canvas does at 100% zoom, and again at 300% zoom, where a smaller picture shows first. The tooth pictures are a deliberately harsh test: the bare surface under a low lamp on the left half, a soft pencil rubbed over it on the right half. The app itself lights paper far more gently.
