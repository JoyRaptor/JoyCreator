Rotation dial — 4 candidates

Drag a dial to rotate (it keeps winding — keep going past one turn). Tap the number to type: 720, -45, 16x all work, same grammar as the app. Double-tap a dial to reset to 0. Positive wraps stack white; negative wraps stack amber.

Pose previewtouch any dial — this card shows what its value does to a real object.

1 · AE angulator

Needle + wrap tape at one radius — overlaps brighten. JoyRaptor's After Effects reference.

0 720° 16x -45°

Tape = one arc per full turn, same radius, ~14% alpha each. 1.5 turns = one gray band; 10 turns ≈ white.

2 · Turn rings

Each completed turn is its own ring stacking inward; big turn count in the middle.

0 720° 16x -45°

You can COUNT turns like tree rings — no alpha pile-up needed to read 10 turns.

3 · Jog wheel

The whole wheel rotates under your finger, like a jog/shuttle dial. Notch stays fixed.

0 720° 16x -45°

Ticks make small nudges readable; wrap tape rings the outside.

4 · Spiral reel

The tape itself unrolls: one loop per turn, spiral growing outward, head dot = needle.

0 720° 16x -45°

Where loops overlap in angle they brighten — winding readable as ribbon thickness.
