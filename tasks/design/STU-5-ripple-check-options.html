When an edit changes the video's length

Your pick on 11-27: at a transition the two clips overlap, so the video gets shorter, the way the export already works. Trimming, a speed change, a longer loop and deleting a clip change the length too. Each time, the question is what happens to the layers above the video that start after that point: pictures, sprites, text and sounds. Moved with the clips, they stay on the same moment of the video. Left where they are, they keep their clock time and land on a different moment. Either way the whole change is one Undo.

True size on the Note 9's width, in Studio's colours. Everything you pick on the right plays here. Tick "don't ask again", answer, then do the edit again to see row E.

The edits it covers

  • Adding a transition, making one longer or shorter, or removing it
  • Trimming the start or end of a clip
  • Changing a clip's speed
  • Making a loop or ping-pong longer or shorter
  • Deleting a clip, or adding one in the middle

Layers that span the point (start before it, end after it) are not moved; only ones that start after it are. Nothing changes in Studio until your picks are built.
