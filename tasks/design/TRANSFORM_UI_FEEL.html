OPTION B — V6YOUR NOTES

Smart handles, no modes

Every handle starts as Scale — an ordinary right-angle box. The shape and colour of a handle tells you what it will do before you touch it. Press and hold any corner or any edge for half a second to change that one handle's job, or to flip. Bend is no longer a handle job — it is a net you switch on over the top, and the ordinary handles keep working underneath it. One finger inside pans. Two fingers scale and rotate about the point between your fingers.

Handle vocabulary — corner · edge
Try this: turn the bend net on, pull a blue dot, then drag an amber edge triangle to resize — the bend stays. · Hold an edge → fold flips the whole shape over that edge like a page. · Two fingers on a corner of the picture: the crosshair is what it pivots on, and it stays under your fingers. · The amber spin arc floating above the top edge is pure rotation — it cannot move or resize the picture, only turn it about its own centre. · Shove a blue dot right into the middle and let go: it hops to the outside of the point it drives, still on its hairline, so you can always grab it back. · While you drag, a faint chip near your finger reports the live number (scale %, degrees, pixels) and fades away on release — the panel line above stays as the persistent state. · Loupe parks a 2.2× window in whichever corner is furthest from your finger, so a fat thumb never hides the point it is placing.
Bend drops a 3×3 net over the picture and each square is drawn as its own flat four-point patch — a hard bend shows faint creases the real GPU mesh would smooth out. The trapezoids are real 4-point perspective, not faked.

What is being tested

  • Does bend now stack instead of fight? Bend is a separate layer. Scaling an edge no longer wipes it — the bend is stored in the picture's own space and rides along.
  • Can you tell what a handle does without touching it? Amber square = keeps your angles, green = semi-fluid lean, red circle = goes anywhere, blue = fluid net.
  • Is the ink light enough? Thin lines, small glyphs — but the invisible touch pad around each one is still 48px.
