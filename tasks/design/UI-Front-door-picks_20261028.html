Front door picks

The lobby has to say, in five seconds, what you can make here: draw and paint, edit a video, make a music visual, animate a character, record. Each one one tap away. Here are three whole ways to do it, then the parts you can swap between them.

00

Today, for comparison

The lobby as it is built now, drawn true size. This one is not a pick. Next to it, what the code says is wrong.

1
No Draw, no music visual in New

The New row is Recording, Project, Character, Import. To draw you have to find Joy Brush, which is the last word on the dial.

2
"Character" goes to the wrong place, sometimes nowhere

It opens Sprite Lab, not Avatar, and only if you already have a project. With none, you land on a project list. Avatar has the same catch. "New" in Viz Lab also drops you on the project list, though Viz Lab needs no project.

3
The bottom words mislead

Finder opens the old forensics Lab. Bits is Capture again. Packs and Setup both open Settings. Remote is the old streaming screen.

4
Recent work misses three kinds

The recent row knows projects, drawings, recordings, photos and exports. It has no sprite sheets, no characters and no Viz Lab looks, and the Library has no Looks shelf.

5
Joybot says nothing to a newcomer

His line only appears when you have recordings. A brand-new user sees no greeting at all.

+
Fixed since the 10-28 audit

Import now really opens your phone's files and brings them in, and every way to the trash now opens Recently deleted.

01

Three directions

Tap a phone to pick it. Flip the switch to see each one as a brand-new user sees it, which is the five-second test.

Picked
1

Marquee, made clear

WHYThe smallest change. Everything you already know stays put; the New row just says the five makes in plain verbs, and every room says what it is for.

WHYKeeps the big hero and the dial, which demo videos love.

COSTFive verbs in one row only fit as short words (Video, Music). Joy Brush is still the last word on the dial, so the hero opens on Studio and a newcomer can still read "video app" first.

Picked
2

What are you making?

WHYThe question is the answer: five big friendly tiles, the way an iPhone app would greet you. Nobody has to learn what "Studio" or "Viz Lab" means.

WHYCarry on sits on top for anyone coming back, so returning is still one tap.

COSTThe dial and the big hero go. Sprite Lab moves inside Animate, one tap deeper. The biggest rebuild of the three.

Picked
3

Five doors

WHYEverything is on one screen with nothing to swipe: each band says what it makes, shows your last piece there, and has its own New. Bands never move, so your thumb learns them.

WHYRecent work is sorted for you: each kind lives in its own band.

COSTNo room for a mixed recent row or a big hero picture. Only the newest piece of each kind shows; older work is in the Library.

02

Swap a part

Each part below fits any direction. Tap one option per part. The switch above (coming back / brand-new) changes these too.

03

Your mix

Your direction with your parts in it. Anything you have not picked uses that direction's own choice.

04

True whichever you pick

These come with every direction, so they are not choices.

1
A make never lands on a list

Animate, Sprite sheet and Edit video start straight away. If they need a project behind the scenes, one is made quietly and named for you.

2
Animate asks once

Puppet (Avatar), sprite sheet (Sprite Lab) or frame by frame (Joy Brush). One small sheet, then straight in.

3
Recent work learns three more kinds

Sprite sheets, characters and Viz Lab looks join the recent row, and the Library gets a Looks shelf. Every card opens the thing itself, and a recording plays.

4
Nothing disappears

Remote stays (it is a launch feature). Finder's Lab and the old screens stay reachable, just not on the front door. Joybot's "take me there" goes to the new screens, not the old ones.

5
Same look as today

Old black, zinc greys. Grey is quiet, colour is what you tap. Pills are flat. Frosted parts stay see-through. A chosen chip is solid cyan with its icon cut out. Studio's New is Studio green.
