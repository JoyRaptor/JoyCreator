What is real here and what is not

  • Joybot is drawn from his real sprite sheets: his tumble for flying, arranging-clips for pulling and holding, discarding-clip for the slap, salute before he flies home.
  • The two rooms are sketches of JoyBrush and Studio, not screenshots. On the phone the show uses pictures of your real screens.
  • The warps are written the same way the phone's shaders are, so the motion is a fair guide. The speed is not: nothing here has been measured on the Note 9.
  • JoyBrush's purple-blue is the app's own. Studio's amber is a stand-in for its room colour.
  • The new room comes in from the left because Studio sits to the left of JoyBrush on the lobby dial.
