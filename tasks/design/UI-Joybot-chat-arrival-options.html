Joybot's arrival

Tap Joybot in the lobby and he comes off that button himself: out of it, a tumble, head first through the air, and a cartoony landing in his corner at the lower left, where your chat drawer has a place made for him. The drawer, its top bar and its title are designed around that one flight, so it reads as one arrival, not a character dropped into an old screen. Every phone here is true size and the Joybot in it is the real sprite sheet at its real speed.

04

True whichever you pick

These come with every direction, so they are not choices.

1
He always animates

His frames, his hover, his sway and his squash play whatever the phone's animation setting says. Only the drawer itself follows that setting.

2
You can type the moment you tap

The drawer and the keyboard are ready within 240 ms; his flight finishes over the top of a working screen and never holds up a reply or a tap.

3
He makes his speech bubbles

Each new answer grows out of the tail above his head, and he plays his talking frames while it appears. Your bubbles drop to the send button on the right.

4
Newest at the bottom, beside him

Older messages scroll up; the newest answer always sits just above him. He is small, so he never covers words.

5
The way back is the way in, backwards

Close the chat and he flies back up into the lobby button with a little hop off his spot, and the drawer goes the way it came.

6
Same look as the lobby

Old black, zinc greys, colour only to guide. Drawers are never opaque; Frost only adds blur. No stock Android icons. Every button has a hover label.
