Lines that stay lines

Pens, inks, markers, plain pencils and fills will stay editable lines on any layer, beside paint that turns into pixels. This page decides how you select, nudge, reshape, slice and recolour a line, and where the two "to pixels" buttons live. Pick a whole direction, or mix parts below. Every scene is drawn at true size. When you're happy, press Copy picks and paste the line back to me.

"I can select a line, I can nudge any part of it with my little finger to nudge it, and it doesn't smudge the pixels, it actually moves the underlying vector line while keeping all the pressure, velocity, and tilt." The owner, on Concepts (brief §3, Q13)
