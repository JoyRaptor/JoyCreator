Motion Blur: the card

Motion blur is Studio's own effect: fast moves smear the way a real camera sees them. These are mockups of the card at phone width, built from the app's own drawer parts and sizes. Tap one choice in each row; the line at the bottom gathers your picks to send back. Where I would choose, the option says my pick, but it is your call.

✓ Your picks, put together

Picked 2026-11-27: A1 B3 C1 D1 E1 F1, with B1's frame labels on the B3 strip. No separate Long trail mode: the strip simply reaches further back, a Streak preset gets you there in one tap, and Fade and Copies live in More, where they work on short blurs too. The card is always a header and three rows.

…and the rest of F1's rows, only where they apply.

Fade and Look work on every blur, not just long ones: a 180° blur with Fade gives a comet tail, Copies at 180° gives a stuttery strobe. Fade 0% and Smear are a real camera, so most people never open this.

No mode, no tick box, no rows that appear and disappear on the card. Under the hood the engine switches from the shutter method to real earlier frames past two frames; you never see that switch.

A Where does it live?

Today Codex built it as its own tool button with a pop-up box. The other effects all live in an object's Effects stack, on a clip, a picture, a sprite or an effect layer.

B How the open card reads

The shutter is how long the camera "looks" at each frame. A wider shutter means a longer smear. All three have the four presets; they differ in how you set the shutter itself.

C Studio's colour on the card

Today an effect card takes its drawer's colour: grey on an effect layer, aqua on a clip, pink on a sprite. Motion blur is Studio's, so where should the aqua-to-lime show? (The house rule: gradient means an action, flat means identity.)

D When a thing can't be blurred yet

Some objects aren't covered yet (for example a picture with a colour key, or a bent sprite). A knob that does nothing is worse than none: what should you see?

E Seeing the difference

Blur is subtle when paused. How do you check it is doing what you want?

F The deeper controls

Samples, quality, ignoring tiny jiggles, a cap on long streaks, and for filmed video how motion is tracked. Most people never need them.
