Try it

Your locked design, working

  • 1Tap the nose line. The row sits above it in the paper's colour; the rotate handle hangs below. The strip's colour and brush ring pulse: they act on the line too.
  • 2Tap the neck hatching, then drag the count ‹ 1 / 7 › sideways to walk through every line under that spot.
  • 3Tap a corner of the box: it fills. Drag it: that corner moves alone and the thin lines show where the edges run. Tap a second corner and they move as a pair.
  • 4Long-press a corner for the Studio's ring: Scale, Free, Tilt, Bend, flips and Reset.
  • 5Loop across the mouth without enclosing it. It takes paint. Tap Lines: it takes the lines it touches.
  • 6Turn on ＋ and hold a finger still (Mouse is: Finger) to use the crosshair: it adds.
  • 7Nudge a line, then look at it close up with Zoom 4×: it stays a curve.

Mouse is

On a computer: the mouse acts as the S Pen (it hovers), and dragging with the right button is the S Pen button. Switch "Mouse is" to Finger to feel the finger rules. On the phone: the S Pen is the pen and your fingers are fingers. With something selected, two fingers anywhere move it (your P8). A two-finger tap is Undo. Scroll this page by dragging outside the mockup.

One finger now pans by default once the pen has been seen (your P10 note); the Select tool's menu (tap Select again) switches it to selecting. Lines here are drawn more simply than Joy Brush draws them.

Behaviour
    
        
  • One press, one undo. Picking, cycling, scrubbing and changing chips are never undo steps.
    • 
        
  • Tap outside ends it. A tap on empty space inside the box does nothing.
    • 
        
  • The pen keeps its tool. The finger and the S Pen button handle the selection.
    • 
        
  • Every guess keeps a way back on screen, and ＋ applies to every way of picking.
    • 
        
  • Lines keep their pressure, tilt and speed through every move, scale, turn, flip and warp.

Look
    
        
  • On the canvas, controls wear the paper: white or black icons by brightness, thin lines, no dark pills.
    • 
        
  • Rotate below, actions above. A finger never covers what it turns.
    • 
        
  • Selected means cyan. Delete is the one red mark.
    • 
        
  • Every control has a hover label, so the S Pen can explain the screen.
    • 
        
  • Fades are slow and smooth, never a pop.
