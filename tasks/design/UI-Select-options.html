Joy Brush Select

Three ways to pick, move and reshape what you have drawn, built on Concepts' grammar and the Studio box you already signed off. The phone below works: draw with your S Pen, tap a line, tap the same spot again, loop around things, drag the box, push a line around. It is drawn at true size, so on your Note 9 one dp here is one dp in Joy Brush. Every choice further down changes this same screen.

Try it

Draw, tap, loop, push

  • 1Tap the nose line. The box and the actions appear. Tap the same spot again to take the line under it.
  • 2Hover the pen over the hatching on the neck. The line a tap would take glows; hover on the line you just took and the glow shows the next one.
  • 3Drag a loop around an eye. The lines it will take light up while you draw. Then flip the chips at the bottom: lines or paint, whole or touching, this layer or all.
  • 4Loop the blue patch top left. No lines inside, so it takes the paint. Drag it away.
  • 5Pick a line and tap Nudge. Push part of it. Zoom in and push again: the ring stays the same size on screen.
  • 6Drag a blue tip dot to bend the end of a line. Every change is one Undo.
  • 7Hold the S Pen button and loop with the Brush chosen. You get a lasso from any tool. With the Brush and a paint selection, the pen paints inside the loop and a finger moves it.

Mouse is

On a computer: the mouse acts as the S Pen (it hovers), and dragging with the right button is the S Pen button. Switch "Mouse is" to Finger to feel the finger rules. On the phone: the S Pen is the pen and your fingers are fingers. Two fingers zoom the page or move the selection. A two-finger tap is Undo. Scroll this page by dragging outside the mockup.

This is a sketch of feel, not the engine: lines here are drawn more simply than Joy Brush draws them, and the Studio box's long-press role ring, Tilt, Free and Bend are shown in the scenes below rather than built here.

My recommendation
Concepts, with five fixes

    

      
Why the actions sit by the box
With the S Pen, the shortest trip is to the thing you just picked. The popup sits under the box, because the box's rotate arc already sits above it. When the box is near the bottom, the popup jumps above. It hides while you drag and comes back when you lift.

      
Why the rest fades
In dense hatching, a cyan glow around one line among twenty is hard to read. Fading everything else toward the paper makes the picked line the only strong mark on screen, which is what Concepts does by default. It fades toward the paper colour, so it works on black and on white.

      
Why the chips re-read the loop
A loop is a guess. Concepts makes you set Partial, Layers and Lock before you lasso. Here the loop is kept, and the chips change what it took after you see it. A wrong guess costs one tap instead of a redraw.

      
Why the finger gets a crosshair
The pen already shows its target by hovering. A finger hides the line it is on, so holding still for a moment lifts a crosshair above the fingertip. A quick drag still draws a loop.

Rules every option keeps

    

      
Behaviour
    
        
  • One press, one undo. Picking, cycling and changing chips are never undo steps.
    • 
        
  • Tap outside ends it. A tap on empty space inside the box does nothing.
    • 
        
  • The pen keeps its tool. The finger and the S Pen button handle the selection.
    • 
        
  • Nudge is measured on screen, so it reaches the same distance at any zoom.
    • 
        
  • Lines keep their pressure, tilt and speed through every move, scale, turn and flip.
    • 
      

      
Look
    
        
  • The box is the Studio's: amber handles, the rotate arc 46dp above, the role ring on a long press.
    • 
        
  • Selected means cyan. Delete is the one red mark.
    • 
        
  • Floating things are see-through, about half, with Frost as blur only.
    • 
        
  • Every control has a hover label, so the S Pen can explain the screen.
    • 
        
  • dp values stay as drawn on bigger screens.
