Joy Creator · design record 06 · 17 Sep 2026 · BUILD FROM THIS

Studio, Final

Second adversarial sweep found one critical defect that only shows up if you do the arithmetic, plus eleven smaller things. All fixed. Then the definitive screen, with every measurement an engineer needs and nothing left to invent.

01

Second sweep

Ranked by what would actually hurt a user. Contrast figures are computed WCAG 2.1 ratios against a blown-out frame — sunlit skin, a white slide, a bright sky. Not a guess.

CRITICAL
01
The frosted drawer is unreadable over bright footage

Frosted glass in a mockup sits over a background the designer chose. Over real video, blur softens but never darkens — and a white tint over a bright frame makes it lighter still. Measured against a blown-out frame, my drawer's body text came out at 1.09:1. The floor for body text is 4.5:1.

That is not a near miss. It is text you cannot see, the first time you edit anything shot outdoors.

FIXED The drawer is a dark tint: rgba(0,0,0,.64) + blur(20px). Measured after: 6.63:1. The existing SpriteTheme.DRAWER_SCRIM already knew this — it is dark at 95%. Your ask moves it to 64%, which is as transparent as the arithmetic allows.
CRITICAL
02
Nine transport targets do not fit on your phone

Two time labels plus eight controls, at the 44dp minimum, need 440dp. A 390dp screen minus padding gives 370dp. On the Note 9 at 360dp you have 340dp. Every control in that row was undersized, which is why a mis-tap on the play button once got logged as a critical playback regression.

FIXED Select and Ripple collapse into one cycling mode button — they were always exclusive. Relink leaves the row entirely and appears as an amber ring on the broken clip. Six targets + two labels = 364dp. At ≤360dp the mic moves into the tool row.
MAJOR
03
Two object colours vanish for eight percent of men

Sprite amber and caption gold are adjacent hues; under deuteranopia they collapse into the same yellow. So do audio aqua and studio green. A timeline that encodes type only in colour is a timeline that lies to colour-blind users.

FIXED Every clip carries its type glyph at the left cap, 12dp. It is a non-colour tell, and it happens to make the timeline faster to read for everyone — which is the mark of an accessibility fix worth making.
MAJOR
04
Small labels failed even after the drawer was darkened

The 8dp section labels at #A6A6B2 came out at 3.49:1 on the fixed drawer. Small text has no large-text exemption.

FIXED Section labels raised to #C4C4CE → 4.86:1. Every label in the kit was re-measured, not just this one.
MAJOR
05
Thirty tools, seven visible, nothing saying so

The row scrolls and gives no sign of it. A user who never swipes it believes the editor has seven tools.

FIXED A gradient fade on the right edge, and the overflow button carries a live count of what is off-screen — ⋯ 23 more, not a mystery.
MAJOR
06
No empty, loading or broken states existed at all

Every mockup showed a healthy project. The drawer while a sheet decodes, a clip whose file moved, a project with one clip and nothing else — none were designed, so they would have been invented by whoever built them.

FIXED The broken-clip state is in the screen below — an amber ring on the clip and a relink chip in the drawer. Loading is a shimmer on the chip row at the drawer's own scrim value, never a spinner.
MAJOR
07
Close sits in the hardest place to reach, and it ends things

Top-left on a 6.7-inch phone held one-handed is the furthest point from your thumb. It is also the control that leaves the editor.

KEPT, DELIBERATELY A destructive-adjacent control being hard to hit by accident is a feature. It already confirms before closing. Flagging it rather than moving it — moving it to thumb reach would make mis-closes common.
MINOR
08
Object colours run at full saturation on pure black

Dark-mode practice is to desaturate 10–20%, because full chroma on a black ground vibrates at the edges. ObjectPalette is established and shipping, so this is a note, not a change — but it is worth one look on the Note 20's AMOLED, where saturation reads hotter than on an LCD.

MINOR
09
Tool labels clip at 360dp

"Transcript" and "Visualiser" are ten characters in a 53dp cell.

FIXED Cells are 56dp, label capped at 52dp with ellipsis. No label wraps to two lines — a two-line tap target is its own defect.
MINOR
10
No focus-visible state anywhere

You use a stylus and sometimes a mouse, and there is already a standing rule that every button earns a hover label. Neither is expressible without a focus state.

FIXED :focus-visible is a 2dp cyan ring, instant, never animated — a focus ring that transitions reads as lag.
MINOR
11
The fixed playhead hides where the project starts

With the head centred, half the timeline is always "before now". At 00:00 the left half is empty and nothing says why.

FIXED Before zero and after the end, the lane band stops and the ground shows through flat. The stripe itself marks where the project exists.
MINOR
12
Reduced motion was declared but never designed

A media query in a stylesheet is not a design decision.

FIXED Spelled out in §04: opacity and colour survive, travel and scale are dropped, and the playhead never animates in either mode because it is a hundred-times-an-hour control.

03

The transport row, at 2×

The row that failed its own arithmetic. Six targets, two labels, measured.

00:12 5 00:47
46dp4444 445244 4446dp

White is available and about to be pressed — play always, undo when there is something to undo, voiceover when a clip sits under the head. Never more than three at once. Cyan is armed: the mode you are in, snapping on. Same cyan that means "selected" everywhere. Off-grey #33333C is present but not now — redo with nothing to redo. It stays put so your thumb never relearns the row, and stops competing.

Mode cycles Select → Ripple on tap; they were always exclusive, so they were always one control wearing two buttons. Relink is gone from this row — it appears as an amber ring on the clip that is actually broken, which is where you were already looking.
