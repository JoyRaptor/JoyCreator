Wide screens

Three screens need your eye before anyone builds them for a tablet: Studio's drawers, JoyBrush, and the lobby. Each has a few whole directions to start from, then every part can be swapped on its own. The mock follows the size bar at the top: try the tablet both ways, half a tablet in split-screen, and a phone on its side. Open this on the tablet and press True size to see it at the real size.

Studio · drawers

Drawers beside the picture

On the phone a drawer rises from the bottom and covers part of the picture. On a wide window it can stand beside the picture instead, so you see the whole frame while you change a title or a sound. The drawer's rows stay exactly the phone's size. Tap a tool cell in the mock to open its drawer; tap it again to close.
