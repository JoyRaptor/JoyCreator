01

The front door

It opens where you left off.

The lobby is a router over every room: a marquee of places, your recents with colour-coded corners, an export that reports as a two-pixel hairline instead of a shouting dialog — and Joybot's single line, which only ever says something it actually verified about your project.

  • Rooms on a marquee

    Swipe the titles, land anywhere. The whole app is one house; the lobby is how you walk it.

  • Carry on

    Recents mix projects, recordings, exports and photos by recency, so the thing you touched last is the thing you tap first.

  • The hairline

    An export in progress is a quiet bar at the very top. It never takes the screen hostage.

  • Joybot's line

    One sentence about your real work. If it has nothing true to say, it says nothing — which is the feature working.

02

Capture

Record anything. Even with the screen off.

A recorder first, an editor second, a studio overall. Joy Creator kept a screen-recorder's years-proven core and pointed it at creators: put your face on a tutorial, run two lenses at once, and let the recording outlive your screen.

  • Screen off, still rolling

    Background video recording that keeps going when the display doesn't. The core that started it all.

  • Face cam over screen recording

    A floating webcam composited while the screen records. The tutorial feature nobody else ships.

  • Two cameras, one take

    Front and back together with dual capture — reactions, unboxings, walk-and-talks.

  • Gets out of your way

    Controls auto-hide. Watermark if you want one. Motion detection on watch, and a live audio visualiser while you roll.

  • Stills on the side

    Photo capture and screenshots, straight into the same library your edits pull from.

03

The editor

A real editor. Not a template machine.

Multi-track layers on a timeline that never stacks two things by accident. Keyframes with easing you choose. Masks. Chroma key proved on a real phone, preview and export alike. And an export that runs in its own process — the app can crash; your render won't.

  • Layers on lanes

    Video, text, sprites, picture-in-picture and adjustment layers — each gets its own lane, guarded so adds never collide.

  • Keyframes + easing

    Arm it, play it, key it at the playhead. Undo is one press, one step — a batch lands as a single undo.

  • Chroma key & masks

    Green screen that survived device proof end to end, with masks over the top.

  • 35+ GL transitions — plus yours

    Drop .glsl files in a folder and they register and run. The pack loader already works.

  • Bend & corner-pin

    Mesh-warp images and sprites, pin text to any quad — all live on the canvas, preview and export agreeing.

  • Text that performs

    Animated type down to per-character odometers, with effects that survive the export.

  • Out-of-process export

    Renders survive the app — and single-frame stills are there when you need one.

04

Animation

Draw it. Rig it. Perform it.

Sprite Lab turns a sheet of frames into named cells and saved animations the engine truly plays. Then go further: trace any flat PNG, drop pins, and puppeteer it — the mesh bends smooth, hair dangles, and your face can drive the mouth.

  • Sprite Lab

    Slice, align, name, save. Animations loop, ping-pong and hold on the timeline — and travel as a .sprite.json any machine can read.

  • Perform, don't program

    Tap a cell while it plays and you're recording a take, right at the playhead. No arming step. Ever.

  • Puppets from a flat PNG

    Trace → pins → bend. Per-triangle depth so a hand can pass in front of the body, and the mesh stays smooth as detail rises.

  • Physics in the rig

    IK, dangle chains for hair and tails, motion smoothing — and it all bakes to keyframes.

  • It watches you

    MediaPipe face landmarks and spectral visemes, computed on-device. The puppet mouths along.

05

Sound & speech

Captions that never touch the internet.

Two offline engines listen — Vosk for timing, whisper.cpp for wording — and a merge pass takes the best of both into one transcript. Then strike the fillers, retime a word, pick a caption style, and export. No key, no upload, no bill. This is the thing nothing else on Android does.

  • Two engines, one transcript

    A genuine merge of Vosk timing with Whisper wording, entirely on your phone.

  • Five animated caption styles

    Pop, zoom, bounce, boxed, hot — pick one and the words do the performing.

  • Fix the take, not just the text

    Filler-word strike and word-level retiming, right on the timeline.

  • Music, measured

    Waveforms with beat and onset detection wired to snapping; loudness normalised to EBU R128.

  • One-tap audio chain

    High-pass → denoise → compress → loudnorm. Plus voiceover, recorded in-app.

06

Joybot

An AI that edits the actual timeline.

Not a chatbot bolted to a sidebar. Joybot wields 55 real tools that mutate your project through a validate-everything-then-apply contract — it proposes, you confirm, and the whole edit lands as one undo step.

  • 55 tools, one timeline

    Captions written from your offline transcript, b-roll found and placed, silences removed — on the real project, not a copy.

  • Proposal cards

    Nothing changes until you say so. Read the plan, tap apply.

  • It sees

    Send a frame, get direction. Vision is built in.

  • Slides without a subscription

    Generated sequences rendered to video on-device — deterministic, no service in the middle.

  • Works while you sleep

    Background jobs survive closing the app. Bring your own key — your prompts go to your provider, never to us. Joy Creator runs no servers.

07

Remote & the rest

Your second phone is a camera.

Another phone on your Wi-Fi becomes a remote camera you frame and fire from where you're standing. Everything stays on your network. Then the Finder's on-device markers surface the moments worth keeping, and the gadget drawer carries the small useful things.

  • Remote camera

    A second phone streams to you over your own Wi-Fi. No cloud, no account, nothing leaves the room.

  • The Finder

    Automatic markers on long recordings — on-device detection finds the keepers for you.

  • Also in the box

    A clock widget, QR and meter gadgets, and whole projects as a zip you can move, back up, or hand to a friend.

Portrait or landscape, both real. The app is built one-handed in portrait and works in landscape — the slots on this page lean portrait because that's how it gets used.
