## 3. How paper and grain work today

**Two grains, one maths** (JB-1.02 / JB-1.05c):
- `core/grain/GrainMath.kt` is the CPU twin of `joybrush/shaders/jb_grain.glsl` (threshold maths) and `jb_grain_sample.glsl` (where textures are read). Change one, change the other, in the same commit.
- **Height-threshold deposit:** `jb_heightCoverage(h, level, edge) = clamp((h − (1 − level))/edge + 0.5)`. Light pressure inks only the peaks; heavy pressure fills the valleys. `jb_grainLevel` adds a tilt-aimed gradient and a radial dome.
- **Tip texture** is sampled in DAB space and turns with the tip. **Paper grain** is sampled in CANVAS (document) space and never moves: `uv = docPx / pitchPx`.
- **Pitch:** `GrainMath.GRAIN_UNIT_PX = 64` doc px per repeat at scale 1, so `pitch = 64 / scale`. This is a fixed physical size, so a bigger brush shows more grain rather than bigger grain.
- **Spec:** `GrainSpec` in `core/brush/BrushPreset.kt` has `enabled, source "cloud"|"image", image, scale, depth: Param, edge, tiltGradient, radial`. **Grain depth is evaluated ONCE per stroke** (`BrushDabber`, first dab; JB-1.05c Decision 10), and the tilt/lean uniforms come from the newest dab of each GPU batch. A per-dab grain level needs a wider per-dab instance record; that is the JB-6.03 Q1 debate. The tuft engine sidesteps it with its own instance layout.
- **Assets:** `joybrush/assets/grain/`, holding only `cloud_256.png`, `cloud_512.png` and `cloud_fine_256.png` (8-bit grey, **tileable**). They are generated by `core/grain/CloudNoise.kt` (fractal value noise on a periodic lattice, seeded hash). They are packaged into the androidkit jar under `/joybrush/assets/grain` and loaded by `androidkit/gl/GrainTextures.kt` with `GL_REPEAT` and linear filtering (check mipmaps). `GrainMath.assetNameFor` only accepts plain `*.png` names.
- **Who uses paper grain:**
  - **Pencil:** `cloud_fine_256`, scale 1.5 (pitch ≈ 42.7 px), depth follows pressure, tiltGradient 0.6.
  - **Sable (tuft engine):** reads the same `cloud_fine_256` at scale 1.5 through `TuftShading.paperAsset/paperPitchPx`, set in `TuftStroke.shading` (`PAPER_TOOTH`, `PAPER_TOOTH_SCALE`). `jb_tuft.frag` uses the height for the **Paper tooth** slider (dry parts break on the peaks; `u_tooth`) and for **stray-hair stutter** (`KIND_HAIR`: a hair catches where `h > stutter`).
  - **Tests pin it:** `DefaultPresetsTest` says "only Pencil (and optionally Smudge) may carry grain".
- **The document's paper:** `core/doc/DocModel.kt` `Paper(color, textureId, textureScale, includeInExport)`. `textureId` is designed but unused. `JbCanvasView.load` REFUSES files with a paper texture ("this screen cannot show yet"). Specs: `JB-2.13b_paper_and_png_export.md` (🟸 Draft), `JB-1.03_cloud_texture.md`, `JB-1.05c_grain_in_the_dab_shader.md`, `JB-1.05d` (image tips and grain, ⚪ Outline, needed by the Photoshop/Procreate/Krita importers, which keep textures but cannot draw them yet, R38/R40).
- **Shared shaders:** `joybrush/shaders/*` also run in the **PC Brush Lab** (`tools/brushlab/BrushLab.html`, WebGL2). Keep them GLSL ES 3.00 and `#include`-able (`ShaderLibrary` expands `#include "x.glsl"`). The Brush Lab does not know the tuft engine yet.
- **Preview tools:** `joybrush/tools/grain_look.js` → `grain_look.png`. In the app, the brush settings' **live preview** and **Test** sheet (`core/chrome/BrushPreviewStrokes.kt`, `TuftTestSheet.kt`) are how the owner judges a change.

## 4. The problem the owner has already seen: visible patterning

The paper repeats on a grid. It is a single **256-px tileable texture tiled every ~43 doc px** with `GL_REPEAT`. At 1:1 on the Note 9, that is the same cloud every ~43 screen px in both directions, and the eye finds the lattice at once. Only three cloud pictures exist, all from one generator, and Pencil and Sable share the same one.

There is a second, smaller source in `jb_tuft.frag`. Its bristle streaks and splits use a cheap fract-hash value noise (`jb_hash21` / `jb_vnoise`) in STROKE space. It can show lattice regularity if it is ever used for paper.

**Directions worth trying (my inference, test them):**
- Break the repeat:
  - two or three samples of the texture at incommensurate pitches and rotations, combined by a non-linear operation (min / weighted, not a plain average, so contrast survives);
  - or stochastic / histogram-preserving tiling (Heitz & Neyret 2018, published research);
  - or a much larger texture;
  - or procedural noise in the shader (hash-based, platform-exact) instead of a picture.
- Real papers: scan the owner's papers (cold-press, bristol, toothy drawing paper) at ≥1200 dpi and make paper presets. The owner can supply scans. The earlier brief (owner's `natural-media-shader-research.md` §2.3/§7) recommends 3–5 presets.
- Zoom: grain defined in doc px is right, but check shimmer when zoomed out (mip levels) and blur when zoomed in. Threshold softness should be ≥ `fwidth(h)`.
- Make paper a **document** setting (`Paper.textureId/textureScale`), so every grained brush reads ONE page. Today each brush names its own picture. That needs the `JbCanvasView.load` refusal lifted and an export story (JB-2.13b).
- Any new knob (paper choice, scale, tooth strength) goes in `BrushKnobs` and shows in the live preview.
