# The curated brush set, the pencil in colour, and the pencil against Proko (brush lane, 2026-11-13)

The owner's ask, 11-13: "we need a curated final brush set ... the essentials that people are familiar with, but
there's so much more that it can do that Photoshop cannot ... the pencils ... not quite up to the bar of the Proko
pencil ... I was not able to change it to be any other color than the black."

**Proof level, read this first.** Everything here is BUILT. The pencil, watercolour and oil brushes were drawn in the
PC lab (`joybrush/lab/media`: the phone's own shaders in a browser, scripted pressure and tilt) and looked at; the
pictures below are those renders. **Nothing here has been compiled into the app or tried on the Note 9 yet**: on 11-13
the build lock and the phone were held by other sessions and free memory was under 1 GB. The Unibrush modes were
written by reading the engine and have not been drawn anywhere. Phone acceptance is owed for all of it.

## 1. The pencil only drew black

**Cause.** A pencil stroke never received the chosen colour (`MediaCanvas.makeLane` dropped it), and it had nowhere to
put one: graphite is stored as grey flakes with a single brightness number per pixel (`paper` store: crush, amount,
brightness).

**Fix.** A coloured pencil lays its colour as a pigment film in the paint planes (where dried watercolour and oil
live), in the same grain and amounts the graphite would have gone down in, and marks that share of the deposit as
coloured so it is not also drawn as grey flakes (`core/media/StickInk.kt`, `jb_media_apply.frag` `u_inkK / u_inkS /
u_inkTint`, one line in `jb_media_render.frag`, one in `jb_media_erase_apply.frag`). So colours layer and mix as
coloured pencils do (yellow over blue reads green). Black is the stick's own graphite, unchanged; a grey lightens the
lead; white is a white pencil. Drawings made before this are unchanged (their coloured share is zero).

**Checked:** lab render of five colours, crossings, grey and white over graphite; black pencil before/after identical
apart from run-to-run noise (1 pixel in 1.7 million differs on the PC, a pixel the old code already mishandled).
`StickInkTest` written. **Not checked:** compiling; the phone; that a coloured pencil stroke's paint planes are saved
and reopened in the atlas (`MediaLayerEngine.dryFrame` now adds `p0` / `p1` to the stores a coloured stroke writes);
coloured pencil under watercolour (a wash may re-dissolve it, as a water-soluble pencil would).

## 2. Pencil against the Proko reference

One pencil, one size, only pressure and tilt change, as in his three screenshots
(`tasks/joybrush/research/proko_pencil_20261113/`). Lab sheet `proko` (`tests.js`), run on drawing paper, bristol,
hot press and cold press.

| What his samples show | Today's Proto Pencil | Proto Pencil 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Upright lines stay whole from faint to black | Break into dashes, badly on cold press | Whole at every pressure on all four papers |
| Six pressure steps, the light ones still visible | The two lightest nearly vanish | All six read |
| Side shading: an even tone with fine speckle | Pale, with a blurred fog at the far edge | An even tone that builds; speckle still coarser than Proko's |
| A dark band along the tip edge, feathering away | Present but weak | Clear dark tip edge, grainy feather |
| Overlapping passes build darker | Barely | Yes (the crossing strokes) |
| Ribbon turns: the dark edge swaps sides | Yes | Yes, stronger |

**How:** the point presses deep and the side barely (`pressDepth` 2.6, `sideEase` 3), graphite goes down in thin even
films (`build` 0.14, `transferExp` 1.15, `leadSoftX` 5.5, `plateau` 3.3), the sheet bends fully onto the lead
(`conform` 1), a longer and wider side (`side2` 14, `face` 1.6). Data only; no shader change.

**Still short of Proko:** our grain is the paper's real tooth and reads coarser than Proko's fine speckle; our
hairlines are a little thicker; on rough cold press the side shading goes blotchy (that is the paper, and arguably
right). A finer grain would need a new knob in `jb_dry_dab.frag` (read the tooth at a finer scale): asked of the
paint-engine session, not done.

Proto Pencil stays beside Proto Pencil 2 so he can compare (his rule).

## 3. The curated set

### Added 11-13

| Name | Engine | Purpose | Why it earns its place |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proto Pencil 2 | pencil | The Proko-matched pencil | Whole lines and even tone from one pencil |
| Glaze | watercolour | Thin staining tint over dried work | Nothing else stays put: every other wash lifts |
| Granulating wash | watercolour | Pigment settles in the paper's dips | Shows the paper engine: skies, stone, rust |
| Bloom | watercolour | Floods and leaves back-runs | The wet-in-wet effect people buy real paint for |
| Rigger | watercolour | Long thin liner | No fine-line watercolour brush existed |
| Mop | watercolour | Wets or tints a whole area | Twice the Wash's reach |
| Edge softener | watercolour | Small damp clear brush | Feathers or loses one edge without flooding it (Water floods, Thirsty lifts) |
| Impasto flat | oil | Thick ridged paint that covers | The only hair brush that lays over wet paint instead of mixing into it |
| Filbert | oil | The rounded flat | The shape between round and flat every oil painter expects |
| Oil liner | oil | Long thin round, fluid paint | No fine-line oil brush existed |
| Chalk, Charcoal, Pastel | Unibrush | Dry sticks with paper grain | Essentials that were missing |
| Technical | Unibrush | Constant-width hard liner | Essential that was missing |
| Airbrush | Unibrush | Small soft build-up spray | Soft was the only spray, at 256 px |
| Ribbon | Unibrush | A flat band that turns with the pen and twists with pressure | Tip shape morph + pen direction: not a Photoshop brush |
| Rainbow | Unibrush | Hue turns with pressure and drifts along the stroke | Colour dynamics showpiece (needs a coloured ink) |
| Foliage | Unibrush | Leaves in one pass | Four generated leaf tips, scatter, colour jitter |

| Dashes | Unibrush | A dashed liner | Line dashes shaped at the lift |
| Hatch | Unibrush | One stroke lays hatching; harder = closer, then crossed | Pressure-picked tip sheets |

**Tried and cut:** Soft blender (oil: laid a cloud of paint instead of blending; the Fan blender covers it), Scumble
and Rake (oil: laid no paint at all in the lab). The lab also showed the existing Fan blender doing nothing over wet
paint: worth an engine look.

- **Pencils (10):** Proto Pencil, Proto Pencil 2, Proto Mix, Proto, Proto 2, 2H, HB, 2B, 6B, Woodless 8B.

**For his eye (a cut list I did not make without him):** the pencil shelf now has five Proto pencils; once he has
compared, three of them (Proto, Proto 2, Proto Mix) look ready to retire. Palette knife 1, 2 and 3 are kept by his
keep-the-old-one rule. "Pencil" (digital stamp) and the realistic pencils share a shelf and a name.

**The rewetting water brush he asked about:** it exists. "Water" is clear water and re-dissolves paint that is not
staining; "Thirsty" lifts paint out; the ⋯ menu "Wash lifts" row sets how much. What decides whether dried paint
wakes up is the paint's own Staining slider. Not confirmed: whether paint already printed from the wet sheet into the
layer can be lifted again.

## 4. Unibrush: true state and the name

- **On for everyone** since 10-31 (`UnibrushActivation.SHIPPING_ENABLED = true`). Several comments and roadmap line 15
  still say "hidden": stale.
- **What it has:** tip shapes with a two-shape morph, soft/hard profiles, directional fade, picture tips (1-16 PNGs he
  can choose), dual tip, tip and paper grain, scatter, size and angle jitter, colour life, curves on pressure, tilt,
  speed, direction and barrel, stroke ends (taper, dashes), stabilizer. A settings sheet opens on hold; his own modes
  are saved.
- **What it lacks against Photoshop:** smudge/mix inside the same brush (smudge is a separate engine), bristle and
  erodible tips, wet edges, a blend-mode control in its sheet, more than three dual-tip modes.
- **Lines or pixels:** pixels by default; with the per-brush "editable lines" switch on, strokes are kept as
  re-drawable lines. The docs disagree on whether every mode passed that on the phone (STATE says all; an older
  evidence note says Ink, Marker and Bristle stayed pixels).
- **Speed (Note 9, 10-30 receipt):** Soft at 256 px about 45 frames a second, at 2000 px about 9. Target was 45 at
  2000 px: not met (the half-resolution buffer, U11, is not built).
- **Open:** recovery after the graphics context is lost, Note 20.

**Name.** Recommendation: **Versa** (his idea, shortened: "Versa brush" in a sentence, "Versa" on the shelf). It says
versatile, is one word like the shelf's other headings, and is not a competitor's brush name that I know of.
Alternatives: **Omni** (same meaning, more generic, widely used elsewhere) and **Joy brush / the Joybrush** (ties to
the wing's name, but then the wing and one brush share a name). Nothing is renamed in code until he picks: the name
shows in about ten places (`BrushDrawerView`, `UnibrushSettingsView`, Joybot's help).

## 5. The three status questions

- **Can editable lines be selected and nudged as in the prototype?** Yes, in the app and reachable: Select picks
  lines; the box moves, sizes, turns and distorts them; "Nudge" bends part of a line with a ring whose size he sets;
  the blue end dots drag a line's ends; recolour, change brush, change weight, duplicate, flip, group. Each is one undo
  step. Agent-checked on the Note 9 (10-30, "Select 21/21"), never accepted by his own hand. Missing: true node editing
  (no control points), a step nudge (code exists, wired to nothing), selecting paint (says "Only lines can"), and
  pencil / watercolour / oil never become lines.
- **Is the utility brush finished?** Working and switched on; not finished: big sizes are slow, context-loss recovery
  and the Note 20 are unproven, and the gaps listed above.
- **Do Photoshop and Krita brush imports work end to end?** No. The readers exist and are well tested (ABR versions 6,
  7, 9, 10; Krita `.kpp` and `.bundle`, pixel engine only; Procreate; MyPaint) but there is no way in for a person: no
  Import row, no file picker, no share target (audit JBX-18, confirmed in code: `MaterializedBrushImports` is used by
  its own test only). An "Imported" shelf already exists and stays empty. Needed: an Import row in the brush drawer
  (the JoyBrush screens session's area), a store for imported brushes, and a phone pass with real files.
