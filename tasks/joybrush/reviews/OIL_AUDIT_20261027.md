- **[code] Stroke ends does NOT default to 0. It defaults ON for hair brushes.** Branch `Paste.kt` (~L73): `val ends: EndsMode? = null`. `loadedEnds` (~L84) = `!blade && ends != EndsMode.CUT`. So `null` means loaded-brush ends are on for every hair brush and the trowel. The branch test `MediaBrushTest` (~L178) asserts `round.media!!.paste!!.loadedEnds` ("a shipped hair brush paints a loaded brush's ends"). The `CUT` value is the old look and must be chosen by the user.
