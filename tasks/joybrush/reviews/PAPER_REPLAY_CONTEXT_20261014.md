## Finding and existing policy

A frozen brush plus `StrokeRecord` is **not sufficient to reproduce the exact paper-dependent pixels made at the original contact after `documentPaper` changes**. It is sufficient to reproduce the procedural stroke inputs (brush definition, raw samples, seed, smoothing and contact zoom), then render them against whichever document paper is current. That latter behavior is the explicit product policy: R10 owner ruling P5 says paper changes apply to every brush; vector ink strokes re-render with the current paper; raster strokes already laid down cannot be recomputed (`tasks/joybrush/research/R10_paper_and_canvas.md:41,166-168`). Therefore per-stroke paper snapshots are not required for the stated ink policy. A claim of identical historical pixels after changing paper would be incorrect.

The distinction matters for the planned recorded-line source cache. `createRecordedScene` captures `documentPaper` and `resolvedPaper` in its renderer context predicate and refuses staged work if those values change (`GlPaintEngine.kt:1619-1632`). That guards a single staging operation from mixing paper contexts. It does not itself rebuild an already-cached line unit after a paper change. The existing `setPaper` code updates loaded resources and invalidates the paper background (`GlPaintEngine.kt:252-261`); its comment explicitly says it does not invalidate painted tiles. If recorded-line activation happens, the P5 policy requires the owning path to invalidate/replay vector sources and recompute their line-crossed looks when paper changes. The current scratch/diagnostic route is not a live view activation path, so this review does not recommend changing ordinary raster behavior.

## What capture and replay actually retain

The diagnostic path freezes the effective runtime brush with `FrozenBrushRegistry.freeze` and uses that detached definition for live dab evaluation (`JbCanvasView.kt:1380-1391`). It starts `StrokeCapture` with frozen brush id, live seed, smoothing amount, `view.zoom`, and color (`JbCanvasView.kt:1472-1474`). `StrokeRecord` serializes id, brush id, seed, smoothing, `screenPerDoc`, samples, color, width scale, sequence, lock and group metadata (`core/.../StrokeRecord.kt:43-56`). `FrozenBrushRegistry` snapshots/validates brush JSON definitions and resolves only document copies; it does not capture paper catalogue entries or image bytes (`core/.../FrozenBrushRegistry.kt:9-19,27-51`). The diagnostic result carries brush, record and actual start values but no `Paper` / `ResolvedPaper` snapshot (`JbCanvasView.kt:323-333,352-363`).

During replay, `prepareRecordedLine` checks current readiness, supported line/brush constraints and current `paperWarnings`; it calls `InkReplay.replay(record, preset)` and then `beginStroke` with the record's color and seed (`GlPaintEngine.kt:1524-1561`). `beginStroke` resolves `StampPass.lookFor` from `loadedPaper?.paper`, the current surface texture, and current fluid texture (`GlPaintEngine.kt:1812-1829`). `StampPass.lookFor` combines frozen brush paper response with the current `ResolvedPaper`: surface identity/texel size and current scale, bite, surface texture, fluid texture, and document-derived paper grain asset/pitch (`StampPass.kt:47-95`; `DocumentPaperGrain.kt:8-17`). Thus a changed paper surface can alter the replayed grain map, physical scale, influence, directional/wet deposition, and watercolor finishing even when the brush and record are byte-identical. `screenPerDoc` preserves smoothing's original zoom input; it does not freeze paper scale or paper texture placement.

`JbContents` stores one document, including its one current `doc.paper`, plus raw strokes, frozen brush copies, pixel slabs, and saved look tiles (`androidkit/.../JbArchive.kt:65-82`). Archive v10 freezes brush definitions, not paper definitions (`JbArchive.kt:102,285-298`). `Paper` stores document-level ids and controls—`textureId`, `textureScale`, `lookId`, `tint`, `show`, `bite`, `light`, and display/export flags—not a per-stroke copy (`core/.../DocModel.kt:75-86`). `PaperState.resolve` joins those ids to the app's packaged paper catalogue (`core/.../PaperState.kt:12-21`; `PaperResources.kt:8-24`). The surface catalogue is an external dependency: its `SurfaceEntry` contains the file, texel/hex/slope parameters, and paper physical/fluid values (`core/.../PaperCatalogue.kt:20-49`). `GrainTextures` loads the catalogue and PNG files from packaged `/joybrush/assets/paper/` resources (`androidkit/.../GrainTextures.kt:100-121`). The document and brush copy do not pin those asset bytes or a catalogue revision. A newer build can therefore resolve a paper id to changed packaged asset content; P5 still says current paper for vector replay, while exact reproduction of an older rendered surface is not promised by these fields.

There is a fail-closed guard for missing/unresolved paper assets in editable replay: `prepareRecordedLine` requires no paper warnings, and `requireRecordedGrainAssets` verifies required document-paper grain and brush tip-grain assets were actually loaded (`GlPaintEngine.kt:1536`; `RecordedGrainAssets.kt:7-18`). Diagnostic capture refuses image brush textures it does not freeze (`JbCanvasView.kt:366-386`), but this is separate from the document paper, which is read from current engine state.

## Compatibility policy and smallest viable choices

1. **Keep the existing P5 semantics (the specified default).** Persist only the current document `Paper`, frozen brush, and raw stroke record. On a paper edit, rebuild the recorded vector line units/look tiles against the new current paper; retain raster pixels as they are. Continue refusing replay when the referenced required resource cannot be loaded, and report the paper warning instead of claiming an exact replay. This preserves ordinary paint and matches the existing owner ruling.
2. **If a future requirement means “recreate what this stroke looked like at contact,” define a separate opt-in compatibility contract first.** It would have to retain the relevant resolved paper/surface parameters and identify the asset content/version (or retain its bytes) per stroke or per immutable paper revision, then replay under that captured context. Merely adding `textureId` to `StrokeRecord` would still not pin the `SurfaceEntry` parameters or PNG bytes. This would change P5's current-paper behavior and needs an explicit owner/spec ruling before schema work.
3. **For diagnostic-only same-contact comparison, pass the captured document paper/resources alongside the diagnostic brush/record or require the paper revision to remain unchanged for the probe.** That is a bounded diagnostic input, not a reason to alter the saved document schema.

No paper state was added to the frozen brush or `StrokeRecord` by this audit. Existing raster replay and ordinary painting behavior remain outside scope.
