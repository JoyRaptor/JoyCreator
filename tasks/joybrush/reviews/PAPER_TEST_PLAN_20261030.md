## 2. Controlled visual/feel canvas comparisons

Use fresh scratch documents only. Keep canvas dimensions, zoom, lamp, brush preset,
pressure/tilt recipe, color and settings fixed. Record exact paper IDs and texel
scale; pair matching Background AND Surface, then test each independently to
separate photographic appearance from physical response.

- Compare a grain-sensitive pencil/dry brush, ordinary stamp/tuft, editable
  watercolor with nonzero paper influence, wet media wash and oil.
- Include upright/detail and tilted/shading strokes, light/heavy pressure,
  overlapping wet marks, long crossings and close views of tile boundaries.
- Inspect grain, pooling, whiteness/tint, fibers, repetition and seams. Compare
  the same crumple at2048/1024/512 without changing other parameters.
- Test switch-away/back, undo/redo/cancel, selected line movement/recolor and
  media settling. Verify scratch save/reopen and export Include/None behavior;
  frozen source/replay and recovery must preserve their documented paper semantics.
- Preserve full path/length/SHA manifests around automated probes. Keep owner
  drawings untouched. Screenshots and numerical agreement do not replace owner
  stylus/creative acceptance; record rejected and approved papers explicitly.

## 3. Compression benchmark after the reference is evaluated

Current master material payload205722497bytes;10x smaller is a desired target,
not a promised quality-preserving result. Preserve immutable full masters and
compare representative crumple, smooth, woven and translucent papers first.

Separate disk compression from GPU formats. Test storage candidates from the
[codec research](PAPER_CODEC_OPTIONS_20261029.md), including decoder APK overhead,
Note9 compatibility, cold/warm decode/transcode time and transient CPU/GPU memory.
Height, slopes, independent height-squared and fluid channels are numerical data;
alpha is not always opacity. Require exact channels for lossless candidates and
explicit brush-response/visual error limits before accepting lossy experiments.
Do not infer GPU savings from a smaller JPEG/AVIF/JXL file alone. Procedural or
hybrid detail is a separate visual candidate, not an exact replacement by assumption.
