# Unibrush D3: four-point tip and A/B morph

Status: engine verified, ready for Lead review on codex/unibrush (2026-10-19).
This is not app or phone acceptance.

## Geometry contract

`tip.quad` is an explicit opt-in. Omission retains the old superellipse/taper arithmetic,
including recordings made before Unibrush. Forcing arbitrary old superellipses through
circular fillets would change their pixels; opening an old brush must not do that.
The D9 shape editor can initialize a four-handle shape when the person edits its shape.

Four ordered points lie in normalized -1..1 tip coordinates. Crossed contours use even-odd
fill. Consecutive duplicates and straight/backtracking vertices are removed before rounding;
an erased retraced edge must not become a hairline. Fewer than three points is empty.
Zero signed area alone does not mean empty: a bow tie has two filled lobes.

At each corner the tangent distance is `roundness * .5 * min(adjacent edge lengths)`.
The trimmed edges meet exact minor circular arcs, with concave corners adding their cap
and convex corners losing it. The default square at roundness 1 takes an exact circle path.
A rectangle at full roundness becomes a capsule. Aspect then makes elliptical nibs;
the existing facing angle, taper, anchor, radial profile and directional fade apply afterward.

Near-straight corners use a stable half-angle formula. Arc distances use a rationalized
physical expression, avoiding huge-center subtraction and division by tiny radii.
Normalized spans below 1e-7 are empty; duplicate distance and radial normalization have
the same numerical floor. Cap triangles with doubled area <=1e-14 stay sharp. These guards
are far below a visible pixel even at the maximum brush size, and avoid denormal/NaN
ghosts on the GPU. The CPU prepares this geometry once per immutable resolved dab.

## Morph and transport

The existing `Param` evaluator supplies t from pressure, tilt, speed or a custom curve.
Points, rounding and aspect interpolate per dab. The two radial profiles blend actual
coverage, including two independent custom LUTs. Missing tilt in the standard additive
recipe means shape A. Morph does not consume random draws, preserving other jitter.

The original 17-float-per-dab prefix is unchanged. Optional tails follow the entire prefix:
2n fade floats, then 12n quad floats. Attributes 9/10 carry points, 11 carries roundness,
normalization, profile mix and kind. Every draw disables/resets its added attributes.
The shader also handles the OpenGL disabled-attribute default `(0,0,0,1)` explicitly;
otherwise older callers could accidentally read an empty shape. Two stroke-constant profile
uniform/LUT sets accompany the per-dab mix, with legacy resets between strokes.
