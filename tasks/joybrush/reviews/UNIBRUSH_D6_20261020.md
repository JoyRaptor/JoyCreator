# Unibrush D6: dual-tip masking

Engine checkpoint on codex/unibrush; application integration and Note9 acceptance remain pending.

Brush v17 adds nullable tip.dual: one static four-point shape or one frozen PNG,
scale, spacing, scatter and multiply/subtract/min modes. The secondary lattice lives
inside the main nib and follows its angle, anchor, aspect and taper. Seeded cell
randomness is independent of all existing dab/color/image/scatter streams. It is
baked once per stroke into an immutable256x256 R8 mask, shared by its dabs. The exact
recipe and seed are checked; stale or missing masks refuse rather than disappear.

All periodic source copies intersecting the bake are included, including wide1x1
bilinear support. Complete candidate/pixel/work admission precedes output allocation.
The default interactive allowance is16million samples,16384 candidates and81920
owned pixels; a deliberately larger oracle test supplies an explicit64million-work
allowance to verify extreme support. There is no truncation or nearest-cell cutoff.
Subtract is arithmetic max(primary-secondary,0). All three modes only remove coverage,
after primary shape/profile/fade/image and before grain, flow, cap and color moments.

StampTipResolver admits the complete ordered primary-plus-dual PNG closure before
either decoder allocates. Archives preserve a secondary image even with a procedural
primary. One bounded decoded brush and one stroke bake are retained. Replay after
frozen JSON/raw codec roundtrip produces identical dabs, mask bytes and admitted output.
Old placement RNG, fields and the17n instance prefix remain unchanged.

The GPU uses a separate REPEAT/LINEAR R8 texture on unit4, no extra instance attributes.
Legacy draws explicitly reset dual mode and bind a safe placeholder. One physical-context
owner handles upload/end/cancel/release and failed-deletion retry. Tests include both
thrown cleanup failures and driver-reported GL errors before ownership is retired.
