## Behavior

The count appears for a tap/crosshair with more than one ranked line. Its ordinal follows
StrokePicker.rank, matching the same-spot tap cycle. Tap selects the next candidate;
horizontal drag uses the approved rounded12dp step and wraps in either direction.
The22dp paper-backed cyan-outline pill has a44dp contact target, a plain hover label,
keyboard click and accessibility forward/back actions. Ink follows sampled contrast.
It is hidden for pixel/loop selections, an ended selection, nudge mode or a moving box.
Its document anchor projects through the live view transform and its contact target
stays clear of the top/footer. The approved cyan gradient/full pills/borderless chrome
remain locked; the web preview is unchanged.

Add captures one base at DOWN, removing the starting candidate's entire group unit.
Each live step is that unchanged base plus the candidate's whole group; reversing does
not accumulate intermediate candidates. Without Add the base is empty. Selecting writes
no drawing records or history. Cancel/owner lift keeps the last accepted choice, with no
extra cycle. Secondary contacts cannot take over. A refused or hidden gesture ends once
and consumes trailing input; a new DOWN recovers even if the hidden view missed the tail.
Vertical movement suppresses tap cycling. Accessibility cannot open an overlapping touch
session. Changed rank, records or lock/group membership abort instead of guessing.

SelectTool exposes CountPick plus beginCountScrub/scrubCount/endCountScrub. Count metadata
is separate from SelectionState.Pick so an additive selection can still show its ordinal.
SelectionState has narrow atomic countBaseWithout/chooseFromBase helpers. Successful box
edits invalidate the old count; cancelled/failed box edits retain its original context.
