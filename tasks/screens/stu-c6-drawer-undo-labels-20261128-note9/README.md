## Added 11-28 (later): paused-effect redraw, text with Brightness / Exposure (FOUND A FAULT: preview shows no effect, export does)
Setup: "Untitled 8" (white Fit-to-width text on a blue clip), text drawer > Effects > Add effect > Brightness / Exposure (Gaussian Blur refuses on an object with "needs its own pass; put it on an adjustment layer", by design). Playhead paused at 0:00. Dragged the **Exposure** slider to +3, then to -3 (Undo went 3 -> 4 -> 5, one step per drag).
- The drawer's own thumbnail followed the slider (brightened at +3, darkened at -3). **The preview picture did not change** at +3 (`fx_exposure_plus3_paused_preview.png`) or -3, measured: the text region's mean colour and brightest pixel were identical before and after, on a paused playhead and again after playing about 2 s. With the drawer closed and Exposure at -3 the text is still pure white (`fx_exposure_minus3_drawer_closed_preview_text_still_white.png`).
- **The export does apply it:** exported at -3 (`Untitled 8 2.mp4`, 640x360), the text is dark grey, brightest pixel 32 of 255, text area mean 27 (`fx_exposure_minus3_export_frame_text_dark.png`). So the preview and export disagree for an object effect on a text; the preview shows the effect not at all. (A paused redraw cannot be judged until the preview draws the effect at all.)
- Not separated: whether it is Brightness/Exposure specifically, text objects specifically, or an object effect on this build's preview in general. Try the same effect on a picture/PiP and another effect (Invert) on the text.
