## The idea in one line

**The video is the proof.** Every shot of the app is a screen recording made by Joy Creator's own Capture room, and
the video itself is cut in Joy Creator's own Studio on a phone. The first thing the viewer sees is the Studio timeline
holding this very video. The last thing is the same timeline zooming out to show it all, then the promise.

## What the viewer must take away (the words, in order)

1. Edited on a phone, in this app. 2. A whole studio on your phone. 3. Paint. Rig it. Cut it. Captions stay on the phone.
4. Music visuals you build. 5. Titles. Record anything. Ask Joybot. 6. One folder, yours. 7. Free. No ads. No subscription. (web cut)

Rules for the words: one to six words a card; Studio's own text overlay in one weight (white, the app's heavy-to-thin
style) so they match the lobby's marquee; no jargon; never "best", "#1", "new", "download now" on the Play cut.
The owner's voice is plain and a little dry; if a line sounds like an ad, cut it.

## Music and beat notes (no copyrighted music)

**Kind:** instrumental, 120 BPM, 4/4, about 60 s, four-on-the-floor with a clean breakdown on bars 13-14 and one clear
drop at bar 15. Warm electronic or light synth-pop; no vocal (words are on screen), no genre that fights the picture.
It must have a plain stem structure so Studio's waveform shows obvious beat ticks.

**Where to get it free (check the licence page for each track on the day, save a screenshot of it in `tasks/show/licences/`; I did not
verify any individual track):**
1. **Pixabay Music**: a general free-for-commercial-use licence, no sign-up needed to download; search "120 BPM", "upbeat electronic", filter instrumental.
2. **YouTube Audio Library** (YouTube Studio > Audio Library): free for videos on YouTube; some tracks need attribution (it says so per track). Pick "no attribution required".
3. **Free Music Archive**: filter to CC0 only (no attribution, any use).
4. **Your own**: VisLab plays tracker and MIDI files; if the owner or a friend makes a 60 s loop, it is the safest and the most on-theme.
Do **not** use anything from streaming apps, TikTok sounds, or "royalty-free" packs bought under a licence that forbids app marketing.
Also check: the licence must cover **the Play Store video and an ad on social media**, not only a YouTube upload.

**Beat work in Studio (this is a shot):** drop the track on Studio's music lane, let beat detection mark the beats, and
snap every cut to a bar. If detection misses the drop, nudge by hand and say so on the card.

## The edit, in Joy Creator Studio (how to make it)

1. Record every shot as a **screen recording with Capture** (the app filming itself), portrait, on the Note 9, one shot per file, 12 s of handles each side.
2. New Studio project "Sizzle Cut", 1080 x 1920, 30 fps. Music on the audio lane first, beats marked.
3. Drop shots on the timeline in order; cut each to its bar length; use Studio's own transitions (35+ GL transitions; choose three and repeat them, not sixteen).
4. Words: Studio text overlays with the animation presets. Keep one style.
5. Export from Studio to the folder; keep the project in the folder as the source ("the video's project is itself a demo": ship it as a downloadable example project later, **after** the project format is frozen).
6. **Export check** (the owner's rule on STU-B3): watch the finished file, not just the preview, once before it goes anywhere.

**Two cuts:** Save a copy of the project; in the copy swap the text on S01 (web only), S15 and S16 to the Play wording. Do not
build the Play cut by cropping the web one.

## Risks and how this plan handles them

- **The app changes under the footage.** Lobby (DOOR-2), tablet layouts and brush editor are all in flight. Film in a single sitting after the week-3 build on the test track is frozen; reshoot S02 if the lobby changes.
- **A 🟡 feature breaks on camera.** That is why S04 and S11 wait for the owner's OK, and why each has a stand-in.
- **Privacy.** No real names, phone numbers, serials or notifications in frame; no children's faces unless the owner decides; the owner's face only by his choice. Clean status bar (see STORE_SHOTS "Before every capture").
- **Music licence.** One track, licence page saved, same track for both cuts.
- **Quality bar.** Play asks for 80% real in-app footage and the actual experience in the first 10 s; this cut is ~90% app.
- **Joybot honesty.** The shot shows a request the app really carries out, with undo.
